About IDR

The Institute of Digital Risk (IDR) is an industry-led training and deployment institute focused on digital, cyber and technology risk roles.

Our programs combine academic research with real-world industry projects to prepare professionals for modern digital risk challenges.

Our Model

Academy

Bootcamps and certification programs for students and professionals entering the digital risk industry.

Innovation & Incubation

Developing AI governance frameworks, cyber resilience programs, and risk management tools.

Advisory

Helping organisations implement global digital risk frameworks and cybersecurity strategies.

Community

IDR brings together students, professionals and industry leaders to collaborate on digital risk innovation.
